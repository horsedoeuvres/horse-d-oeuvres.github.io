## Is There an Arc Curse?

After being trialed at the bogs of Longchamp, Japanese racehorses never reclaim their former glory — or so goes the superstition. Manhattan Cafe was injured and retired soon after, Satono Diamond became a shell of himself, and Titleholder never won another G1 either... 

This curse is spoopy and cool makes a lot of emotional sense. The only problem is that you have to ignore all the horses who seemed blithely unaware of its existence.

Here is the form of every Japanese Arc runner of the 21st century who returned to give battle at home after. The "Arc curse" seems quite selective in who it afflicts. 

![](/images/arc_curse.png)

The truth is probably less glamorous. Racing is dangerous. Losing is the norm, and winning the exception. It is usually horses in peak form that get sent to the Arc, and expecting them to hold on to it forever is surely asking for too much. The very best horses can — and do — keep winning, but they are few by definition.  It is enough if Japan's racehorses make it there, try their luck, and return home safe and sound.
